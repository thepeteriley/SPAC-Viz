# Code of conduct

SPAC-Viz contributors are expected to communicate professionally, welcome
constructive technical disagreement, respect differing backgrounds and levels
of experience, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and
other abusive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to keep
the community safe and productive.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent
practical.
